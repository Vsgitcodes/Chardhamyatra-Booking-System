About Us

A small team turning a big idea into reality — making sacred travel transparent, fair, and trustworthy through blockchain technology.

Our Mission

Remove the middleman from sacred travel. Every rupee a pilgrim pays should reach the hotel, the taxi driver, the cook — not disappear into platform commissions. We built Kedarnath Yatra so the money flows exactly where it should, verified by the blockchain and no one else.

Our Vision

A future where every Char Dham destination — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath — runs on the same transparent system. Pilgrims trust the journey. Providers trust the payment. The blockchain trusts nothing and verifies everything.

Technology Stack

What we built this with

Solidity

Smart-contract language. The KedarnathBooking contract lives here — packages, bookings, payments, all on-chain.

Ethereum / Ganache

Local blockchain for development. Ganache gives us 10 pre-funded accounts and instant transactions.

Web3.js

The bridge between the browser and the blockchain. Handles wallet connection, contract calls, and event listening.

MetaMask

The wallet. Signs transactions, switches networks, and manages all six role-based accounts seamlessly.

HTML / CSS / JS

Vanilla stack — no frameworks. Every page is a single HTML file with its own CSS. Fast, simple, portable.

Remix IDE

Where the contract was written, compiled, and deployed. Browser-based — zero local tooling required.
