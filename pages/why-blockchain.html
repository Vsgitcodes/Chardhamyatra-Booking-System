Why Blockchain?

Discover how blockchain technology revolutionizes travel booking with transparency, security, and fairness

The Problem with Traditional Booking Platforms

Traditional travel booking platforms charge 15-30% commission, delay payments to service providers, lack transparency, and centralize control. Blockchain technology solves all these problems.

Zero Commission

No middlemen taking 15-30% cuts. 100% of your payment goes directly to service providers through smart contracts. What you pay is what they receive.

Instant Settlements

Service providers receive payment immediately upon marking service complete. No waiting 30-60 days for traditional platforms to process payments.

Immutable Records

Every booking is permanently recorded on Ethereum blockchain. Cannot be altered or deleted. Complete audit trail forever.

Smart Contracts

Automated execution of agreements. No human intervention needed. When conditions are met, payments are automatically released. Zero disputes.

Full Transparency

See exactly where every penny goes. Track your payment flow in real-time on the blockchain. View transaction history anytime on Etherscan.

Decentralized

No single point of failure. No company can shut down or censor your bookings. Runs on thousands of nodes worldwide.

How Our Smart Contracts Work

1

Admin Adds Package

Admin creates a package with services (hotel, taxi, food, meals) and assigns each service to a verified provider address. Smart contract stores all details on blockchain.

2

Customer Books & Pays

Customer pays total amount in ETH. Money is locked in the smart contract (not in anyone's wallet). Transaction recorded on blockchain with timestamp.

3

Services Delivered

Each provider delivers their service (hotel stay, taxi ride, meals). After completion, they mark service as complete on blockchain using their wallet.

4

Automatic Payment Release

Smart contract automatically credits provider's balance. Provider can withdraw ETH to their wallet anytime. No waiting, no manual processing, no disputes.

Security & Trust

Ethereum Network Security

Protected by the world's largest decentralized computer network. Thousands of validators secure every transaction. Impossible to hack or manipulate.

Cryptographic Signatures

Every transaction signed with your private key. Only you can authorize payments from your wallet. No one else can access your funds.

Audited Smart Contracts

Our smart contracts are open-source and audited. You can verify the code yourself on Etherscan. No hidden backdoors or surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need cryptocurrency to book?

Yes, you need ETH (Ethereum) in your MetaMask wallet. You can buy ETH from exchanges like Coinbase, Binance, or WazirX and transfer to your MetaMask wallet.

Is my payment safe?

Absolutely. Your payment is locked in a smart contract on Ethereum blockchain - the most secure decentralized network in the world. No person or company can access it except through the automated smart contract rules.

What if a service provider doesn't deliver?

Service providers only get paid when they mark service as complete using their blockchain address. If service isn't delivered, they can't mark it complete, and the money stays locked in contract.

Can I get a refund?

Refund policies are encoded in the smart contract. For this version, once booked and services start being delivered, the booking is final. Future versions will include partial refund mechanisms.

Why is blockchain better than traditional payment?

Zero commission (traditional platforms take 15-30%), instant payments (not 30-60 days), complete transparency (see every transaction), no central authority (truly peer-to-peer), and permanent records (can't be deleted or altered).
